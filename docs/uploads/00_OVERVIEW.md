# 2026 Strait of Hormuz Crisis — Overview

> **Zoom Level:** Satellite (Level 0)
> **Purpose:** Landscape view of the crisis — what happened, when, and what domains are affected.

---

## What Happened

On **February 28, 2026**, the United States and Israel launched a military campaign against Iran, escalating from failed nuclear negotiations in Geneva and a prior 12-day air conflict in 2025. Iran retaliated by **closing the Strait of Hormuz** to commercial shipping using naval mines, advanced missile systems, and drone strikes. The IRGC issued warnings forbidding passage, launched 21+ confirmed attacks on merchant vessels, and laid sea mines across the strait.

On **March 4, 2026**, the strait was effectively closed. Oil and LNG exports were stranded. On **March 19**, the US military began operations to re-open the strait.

---

## Why It Matters

The Strait of Hormuz is the world's most critical maritime chokepoint:

| Flow | Share of Global Trade |
|------|-----------------------|
| Crude oil | ~20% (20M barrels/day) |
| LNG | ~20% of global trade |
| Fertilizers | ~30% of international trade |
| Helium | ~34% of global supply (Qatar) |
| Aluminum | ~9% of global primary production |
| Neon (for chips) | ~98% of Qatar's exports |
| Methanol | ~33% of seaborne trade |

This is described as the **largest disruption to world energy supply since the 1970s** — and arguably the largest in oil market history. The 1973 embargo removed 4.5M barrels/day; this closure stops ~20M barrels/day.
