# Fertilizer and Agriculture

## Why Fertilizer Is Critical

Modern agriculture depends on synthetic fertilizers (nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium). Nitrogen fertilizers (urea, ammonia, UAN) are produced from **natural gas** — making them directly dependent on [[01_OIL_AND_ENERGY]] flows.

**~30% of internationally traded fertilizers** normally transit the Strait of Hormuz.

---

## Price Shock

| Fertilizer | Pre-crisis (Dec 2025) | Crisis Price (Mar 2026) | Change |
|------------|----------------------|------------------------|--------|
| Urea | ~$340/MT | >$600/MT | **+77%** |
| DAP/MAP | — | >$700/MT | Significant |
| UAN | — | >$400/MT | Significant |

FAO projects fertilizer prices could average **15-20% higher** in H1 2026 if the crisis persists.

---

## Transmission Chain

```
Natural gas blocked (Hormuz)
    │
    ├─→ Gas price spike
    │       └─→ Ammonia production costs rise
    │               └─→ Urea/UAN/DAP prices rise
    │                       └─→ Farmers reduce fertilizer application
    │                               └─→ Lower crop yields → [[03_FOOD_SECURITY]]
    │
    └─→ Physical fertilizer shipments blocked
            └─→ Seaborne fertilizer supply drops
                    └─→ Importing countries face shortages
```

---

## Critical Timing

The closure is **catastrophically timed** for Northern Hemisphere agriculture:

- **US spring planting** (March-May): Peak fertilizer import season. Vessels from Persian Gulf to US Gulf Coast take ~30 days.
- **India Kharif season** (June-September): India relies on domestic fertilizer production powered by imported gas.
- **Bangladesh Boro rice** (February-May): Critical season underway.
- **Sri Lanka Maha harvest**: Currently underway.

---

## Downstream Impact on Farming

- Farmers face a binary choice: **pay 77%+ more** or **reduce application**
- Reduced fertilizer → **lower yields** → reduced supply → higher food prices
- Developing countries with thin margins hit hardest — see [[03_FOOD_SECURITY]]
- Even if strait reopens, **planting windows are fixed** — missed fertilizer = missed season

---

## Country Deep Dives

- **Turkey** (ranked #2 globally for fertilizer vulnerability) — see [[11_TURKEY_FERTILIZER_DEEP_DIVE]] and [[10_TURKEY_EXPOSURE]]. Key facts:
  - ~40% of urea imports from Oman + other Gulf producers (2025 baseline)
  - Gübretaş reported daily farmer demand **doubled** immediately post-Feb 28
  - Government cut urea import tariffs within days of the crisis
  - 2025 drought already depressed yields → compounded shock
  - Spring top-dressing window (Feb–Apr) is **live right now** — the most time-sensitive moment
