# Shipping and Trade

> **Zoom Level:** Region (Level 1)
> **Role in cascade:** Cross-cutting **cost multiplier** — affects ALL domain pages
> **Connected to:** Every domain page (shipping costs amplify all commodity price increases)

---

## The Cost Multiplier Effect

Shipping disruption acts as a **hidden tax on everything**. Even commodities not physically blocked by Hormuz become more expensive because global shipping capacity is reallocated, insurance costs spike, and alternative routes are longer/costlier.

---

## Key Metrics

| Metric | Pre-crisis | Crisis Level | Change |
|--------|-----------|-------------|--------|

## Insurance Crisis

The insurance situation has become a bottleneck of its own:

1. **Major insurers cancelled war risk cover** in the Gulf within days
2. Premiums jumped from 0.02% to **5% of vessel value**
3. Many shipowners refuse to transit even if physically possible — **uninsurable risk**
4. US announced a **$20 billion reinsurance program** to help revive shipping
5. Even with reinsurance, risk premiums add massive costs passed to consumers

---

## Rerouting and Alternative Routes

With Hormuz effectively closed, trade seeks alternatives:

| Alternative | Status | Limitations |
|-------------|--------|-------------|
| UAE's ADCOP pipeline (Abu Dhabi → Fujairah) | Operational | Limited capacity (~1.5M bbl/day vs 20M through Hormuz) |
| Saudi East-West pipeline | Exists | Limited capacity; not designed for current volumes |
| Cape of Good Hope routing | Being used | Adds 2-3 weeks to Asia-Europe voyages |
| Overland routes | Limited | Not viable for bulk commodities at scale |

---

## Cascading Trade Effects

The shipping disruption cascades beyond Hormuz-transiting goods:

1. **Vessel reallocation:** Ships rerouted from other routes to serve rerouted Hormuz traffic → shortages elsewhere
2. **Port congestion:** Alternative ports not designed for redirected volumes
3. **Container imbalance:** Containers accumulate in wrong locations
4. **Just-in-time failure:** Manufacturing that depends on precise delivery schedules breaks down
5. **Air freight surge:** Critical goods shift to air → air freight rates +70%

## Historical Comparison: Shipping Disruptions

| Event | Strait Affected | Duration | Shipping Impact |
|-------|----------------|----------|-----------------|
| 1956 Suez Crisis | Suez Canal | Months | Cape routing; oil shock |
| 1967 Six-Day War | Suez Canal | 8 years closed | Massive rerouting |
| 2021 Ever Given | Suez Canal | 6 days | $9.6B/day in trade blocked |
| 2024 Houthi attacks | Bab el-Mandeb | Months | Red Sea rerouting |
| **2026 Hormuz** | **Hormuz** | **Ongoing** | **Largest chokepoint disruption ever** |
