## The Chokepoint

The Strait of Hormuz normally handles:
- **~20 million barrels/day** of crude oil (~20% of global supply)
- **~20% of global LNG trade**
- **84% of crude through the strait** was destined for Asian markets in 2024

The closure reduced shipments by **>90%** within days.

## Transmission Mechanisms

### Direct effects
- Oil supply physically blocked → price spike
- LNG from Qatar halted (Ras Laffan targeted by strikes) → European and Asian gas crisis
- Strategic petroleum reserves being tapped but insufficient for prolonged closure

### Second-order effects
- Energy costs rise for **all** manufacturing, especially energy-intensive sectors:
  - [[06_ALUMINUM_AND_METALS]] — smelting requires massive electricity
  - [[04_SEMICONDUCTORS]] — chip fabs are electricity-intensive
  - [[07_PETROCHEMICALS_AND_PLASTICS]] — oil/gas is both fuel AND feedstock
- Electricity rationing in East Asia forces priority choices between sectors
- Transportation costs rise across all supply chains → [[08_SHIPPING_AND_TRADE]]

### GDP impact (Dallas Fed estimates)
- **1-quarter disruption:** Global GDP growth falls 0.2 percentage points
- **2-quarter disruption:** Falls 0.3 pp
- **3-quarter disruption:** Falls 1.3 pp (severe recession territory)
